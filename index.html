Every bag starts as a roll of film.

We source HDPE and LDPE film, then slit, cut and seal it into the exact packaging your business runs on — from Yodi sachets and chin-chin nylons to market, pharmacy and cake bags.

Request a quote See the product range

One 25kg roll of HDPE film converts to roughly 1,800 Yodi-sized sachets, or 600 medium market bags, depending on your gauge and size.

Two resins, two jobs

Which film we choose depends on what the bag has to do — carry weight and resist tearing, or seal tightly and stay flexible in the cold.

HDPE — High-Density Polyethylene

Feel
Stiffer, slightly opaque, a faint rustle
Strength
High tear and puncture resistance for its weight
Where we use it
Black, yellow and white market carrier bags; retail packaging

LDPE — Low-Density Polyethylene

Feel
Softer, clearer, more stretch before it tears
Strength
Better seal strength and flexibility, including in cold storage
Where we use it
Yodi and chin-chin nylons, pharmacy bags, cake bags

From film roll to finished bag

1

Film sourcing

We select the right HDPE or LDPE film roll for the job — gauge, colour and clarity chosen to match the finished bag.

2

Slitting & cutting

Rolls are slit to width and cut to the length your bag or sachet needs.

3

Sealing

Edges are heat-sealed to your bag style — flat, gusseted, or sachet.

4

Pack & dispatch

Bags are bundled by size and colour, ready for pickup or delivery.
